# Weather [later]

Needs the Weather app. Sub-header only.

- Rain - "Rainy one. Good day to stay in."
- Rain + evening - "Listen to that rain."
- Snow - "Snow outside. Stay warm."
- Sunny - "Sun's out."
- Hot (over 25°C) - "Warm one. Stay cool."
- Freezing (under 0°C) - "Freezing out. Extra layer today."
- Storm - "Stormy out there. Cozy in here."
- Fog - "Foggy out."
