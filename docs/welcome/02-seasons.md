# Seasons

Northern Hemisphere, starting on the 1st of the month.

## Season (any day)
- Spring - "Everything's starting to bloom."
- Summer - "Long days, slow pace."
- Autumn - "Leaves are turning."
- Winter - "Blanket season."

## Season + time of day
- Spring morning - "Birds are up early again."
- Spring afternoon - "Things should be warming up."
- Spring evening - "The evenings are getting longer."
- Summer morning - "Get the cool air while it lasts."
- Summer afternoon - "Stay cool and drink some water."
- Summer evening - "Open window weather."
- Autumn morning - "Crisp one out there."
- Autumn afternoon - "Perfect hot drink weather."
- Autumn evening - "Dark early. Candle time."
- Winter morning - "Cold one. Take it slow."
- Winter afternoon - "Sun's already heading down."
- Winter evening - "Long night. Stay warm."

## Season moments (time windows)
- Day before a season - "{Season} starts tomorrow."
- First day of a season - "It's the first day of {Season}."
- First week of a season - "{Season}'s just getting started."
- Last week of a season - "Last few days of {Season}."
