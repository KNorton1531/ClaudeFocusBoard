# Holidays (UK focused)

## Stages
| Stage | When | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Build-up | From a start date until 2 days before | Sub-header |
| Tomorrow | 1 day before | Header |
| The day | The day itself | **Takeover**: header AND sub-header |
| The day, by time | Evening / night version of the day | Takeover |
| After | 1 day after (optional) | Sub-header |

**Takeover** (from the old version): on the day itself, the holiday owns both lines.

## Halloween (31 Oct)
- Build-up (from 1 Oct) - "Spooky season. {daysUntil} days to Halloween."
- Build-up (last week, from 25 Oct) - "It's almost Halloween."
- Tomorrow - "It's Halloween tomorrow."
- The day - "Happy Halloween! 🎃" / "Hope it's spooky and fun."
- The day, evening - "Happy Halloween, {name}! 🎃" / "Trick or treaters are out."
- After - "Halloween's done. Bring on the cozy months."

## Bonfire Night (5 Nov)
- Build-up (from 3 Nov) - "A night of fireworks and bonfires is coming."
- Tomorrow - "Fireworks tomorrow."
- The day - "Happy Bonfire Night." / "Remember, remember the fifth of November."
- The day, evening - "Fireworks are going off." / "Wrap up warm if you're heading out."

## Remembrance Day (11 Nov) - respectful, no emoji
- The day - "Remembrance Day." / "Lest we forget."
- 11am - "Two minutes' silence at 11." / "Lest we forget."

## Christmas (25 Dec)

- Build-up (from 1 Dec) - "{daysUntil} days until Christmas."
- Build-up (last days, from 20 Dec) - "Christmas is coming!" / "The festive season is almost here."
- Christmas Eve - "It's Christmas Eve!" / "Nearly time."
- Christmas Eve, night - "Santa's on his way, {name}." / "Best get to sleep."
- The day - "Merry Christmas! 🎄" / "Wishing you a day full of warmth and cheer."
- The day, evening - "Merry Christmas, {name}." / "Time for a film and leftovers."

## Boxing Day (26 Dec)
- The day - "Happy Boxing Day!" / "Leftovers day. No plans needed."

## New Year's Eve (31 Dec)
- Build-up (27 to 30 Dec) - "New Year's Eve is on the horizon." / "{daysUntil} days left of {year}."
- The day - "Happy New Year's Eve!" / "Here's to a bright year ahead."
- The day, 11pm - "Nearly midnight, {name}." / "Almost {nextYear}."

## New Year's Day (1 Jan)
- The day - "Happy New Year! 🎉" / "Welcome to {year}."
- The day, 12am to 3am - "Happy New Year, {name}!" / "You made it to {year}."

- The day - "Happy Valentine's Day! ❤️" / "Spread some love today."

## Pancake Day (changes yearly, 47 days before Easter)

## St Patrick's Day (17 Mar)

## Easter (changes yearly)

- Tomorrow - "It's Easter tomorrow."
- The day - "Happy Easter! 🐣" / "Chocolate for breakfast is allowed."

## Mother's Day / Father's Day [?] (change yearly)
- Tomorrow - "It's Mother's Day tomorrow. Card sorted?"
- The day - "Happy Mother's Day." / "Give her a call."

## Moving dates
Easter, Pancake Day, Mother's Day and Father's Day move every year. Simplest option: type the next 5 years of dates into the holidays file by hand.
